# Making prayer reels with Claude

Claude can turn your prayer and your photos or videos into a finished vertical
video for TikTok, Instagram Reels or YouTube Shorts.

## One-time setup (5 minutes)

1. Open Claude (claude.ai or the Claude app) and go to **Settings >
   Capabilities**. Turn on **Code execution and file creation**.
2. On the same page, under network access, make sure **github.com** is allowed.
   (This is for the voiceover. Without it, videos still work, but without a
   voice.)
3. Download the file **aa-reels.zip** you were sent. Don't unzip it.
4. Go to **Customize > Skills**, click **+**, then **Create skill > Upload a
   skill**, and choose **aa-reels.zip**. Make sure it's switched on.

## Making a video

1. Start a **new chat** and type: **Make a prayer reel**.
2. Claude shows you the two templates and a short form. Copy the form, fill
   it in, and attach your photos or videos:

```
Template: 1
Music: On
Voiceover: Yes
Voice: Female
Text:
Psalm 85:6
Your faith may feel weak,
Lord, will You not *revive* us again
Amen.
Media Assets: Image
```

3. Send it. Your video arrives in about 2–3 minutes (later ones are faster).
   Tap it to download.

## Tips

- **Two templates:** Template 1 shows the prayer line by line, as it's spoken.
  Template 2 shows the whole prayer at once.
- **Verse first:** put the verse reference on the first line (for example,
  *Psalm 85:6*).
- **Template 1: one line = one screen.** Short lines of 3 to 8 words look best.
  Longer lines are split for you; your words are never changed.
- **Highlight a word** by putting stars around it: `*revive*`. It appears in
  the red tag. If you don't mark any, Claude picks the key word for you.
- Full stops aren't shown in the video, so you don't need to remove them.
- **Several photos or videos?** Attach them all; they take turns in the video,
  in order.
- **Vertical photos and videos** look best. Horizontal ones get cropped to the
  middle.
- **Want a change?** Just ask, for example: "Use Template 2", "Male voice",
  "Slower voice", "Turn the music off", "Change the third line to ...".

## If something goes wrong

Claude will tell you what happened and how to fix it. Most often, a setting
under **Settings > Capabilities** needs to be switched on, and then you start a
new chat.
